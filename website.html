01 • The problem

The local discovery gap.

A business can be physically nearby without being digitally discoverable at the moment a customer needs it.

“How can a person in Coimbatore discover what is actually available nearby, connect directly with the local business, and complete the transaction without unnecessary travel?”
📦

Availability

Finding a business does not necessarily reveal whether the required product or service is available now.

🚗

Unnecessary travel

Customers may call or visit multiple businesses before finding what they need.

🏪

Fragmented presence

Local businesses may rely on walk-ins, calls and messaging rather than one digital storefront.

🎙️

Accessibility

Not every customer wants to type or navigate a complex commerce interface.
